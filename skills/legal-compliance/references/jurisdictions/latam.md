## 1. Common patterns in the region

- **Consent is the main legal basis** in most countries (Colombia, Argentina, Peru, Mexico for sensitive data). Brazil and the new Chilean law also accept other bases (contract, legal duty, legitimate interest).
- **The business must prove consent.** Keep evidence of when, how, and to which text each user agreed. See `../consent-records.md`.
- **Sensitive data** (health, biometrics, ethnic origin, religion, sexual life, political opinions, union membership; in some countries also financial data and children's data) needs express consent, often written, and answers must be optional.
- **The privacy notice must be in the local language** (Spanish, or Portuguese for Brazil) and in plain words.
- **International transfers** (for example to US cloud providers) are allowed with conditions: consent, contracts, or adequacy. Name the countries in the Privacy Policy.
- **Consumer laws are strong.** Remote purchases have a legal right of withdrawal ("derecho de retracto" / "arrepentimiento"). Abusive clauses are void. Arbitration clauses and foreign courts usually cannot bind consumers.
- **Refund period across the region**: Colombia 5 business days, Mexico 5 business days, Brazil 7 calendar days, Argentina 10 calendar days, Chile 10 days.

## 2. Colombia

**Data protection ("Habeas Data")**

- Law: Ley 1581 de 2012, Decreto 1377 de 2013 (compiled in Decreto 1074 de 2015). Regulator: Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC), https://www.sic.gov.co.
- **Authorization** must be prior, express, and informed, and the business must keep proof of it (Decreto 1377, art. 8). Valid forms: written, oral, or "unequivocal conduct" (for example checking a box).
- Mandatory documents: **Política de Tratamiento de la Información** (with the required content of Decreto 1377, art. 13: identity of the controller, purposes, rights, the area that handles requests, the procedure, effective date) and **Aviso de Privacidad** (short notice at the point of collection).
- Rights: know, update, correct, delete, revoke authorization, and complain to the SIC. Deadlines: queries ("consultas") 10 business days, claims ("reclamos") 15 business days.
- Sensitive data: optional answers, explicit authorization. Data of minors: only with the authorization of the legal representative and in the best interest of the minor.
- **Registro Nacional de Bases de Datos (RNBD)**: required for companies with total assets over 100,000 UVT (verify current threshold) and public entities. Smaller companies are exempt but must still comply with the law.
- International transfers (Ley 1581, art. 26): allowed to countries with adequate protection, or with an exception such as consent or a contract. The SIC published model transfer clauses in Circular Externa 003 de 2025 (verify). For US hosting, document the transfer and use the model clauses.
- Breach: report security incidents to the SIC through the RNBD within 15 business days after detection (verify).
- Fines: up to 2,000 monthly minimum wages (SMMLV).

**Consumer law**

- Estatuto del Consumidor, Ley 1480 de 2011.
- **Derecho de retracto** (art. 47): 5 business days from delivery of the product or from the contract date for services. For e-commerce, **Ley 2439 de 2024** sets the refund deadline at **15 calendar days** from when the consumer exercises the right, to the original payment method (verify start of the period). Exceptions include custom goods, perishables, services already started with consent, and digital content downloaded (verify full list).
- **Reversión del pago** (art. 51): for electronic payments, the consumer can request a payment reversal within 5 business days in cases of fraud, unauthorized operation, product not received, or defective product.
- **E-commerce duties** (art. 50): seller identity (legal name, NIT, address, phone, email), complete product information, total price with taxes and shipping, order summary before payment, purchase receipt, and a link to the SIC.
- Legal warranty ("garantía legal") applies to all products and cannot be removed.
- Ley 527 de 1999: electronic messages and contracts are valid.
- **Ley 2300 de 2023** ("Dejen de fregar"): marketing contact by phone, SMS, email, or WhatsApp only Monday to Friday 7:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 8:00 to 15:00, at most once per week, and only through the channel the consumer authorized.

## 3. Mexico

**Data protection**

- Law: new **Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (LFPDPPP)**, published 20 March 2025, in force 21 March 2025. The INAI was dissolved. The authority is now the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (verify current name and portal).
- **Aviso de privacidad** is mandatory. The 2025 law requires listing the personal data processed and flagging sensitive data, and no longer requires disclosing transfers in the notice (verify). Typical content: identity and address of the controller, data collected, purposes (separate primary purposes from secondary purposes such as marketing, with a way to refuse the secondary ones), transfers, how to exercise ARCO rights and revoke consent, and how changes are notified.
- **Consent**: tacit consent is valid for general data if the privacy notice was made available. Express consent for financial and patrimonial data. **Express and written** consent (electronic signature or equivalent) for sensitive data.
- **ARCO rights** (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition): answer within 20 business days (verify under the 2025 law).
- Transfers: generally need consent, except listed cases. Communicate the privacy notice to the recipient.
- Breach: notify affected data subjects without delay when the breach significantly affects their rights.
- Evidence: NOM-151-SCFI-2016 sets how to keep data messages (for example electronic contracts) with integrity. Consider it for consent records (verify relevance).
- Fines: up to 320,000 times the UMA, doubled for sensitive data (verify).

**Consumer law**

- Ley Federal de Protección al Consumidor (LFPC). Authority: PROFECO, https://www.gob.mx/profeco.
- **Revocación** (art. 56): for sales outside the establishment, including remote and online sales, the consumer can revoke within **5 business days** from delivery of the product or signature of the contract, whichever is later. Refund the money after the product is returned (verify exact refund deadline).
- E-commerce (art. 76 bis): give the consumer the seller's identity and contact, clear terms, total price, and respect their choice not to receive marketing.
- **Subscriptions and recurring charges** (art. 76 Bis, fractions VIII and IX, reform published 12 December 2025 (verify)): disclose frequency, amount, and charge date; get express, informed consent and keep proof of it; notify at least 5 calendar days before each automatic renewal; offer immediate cancellation without penalty.
- PROFECO keeps a "Registro Público de Consumidores" ("no llamar" list) for marketing calls and messages.
- Contracts of adhesion in some sectors must be registered with PROFECO (verify if the business sector requires it).

## 4. Brazil

**Data protection**

- Law: **LGPD**, Lei 13.709/2018. Regulator: ANPD, https://www.gov.br/anpd.
- Ten legal bases (consent, contract, legal duty, legitimate interest, and others). Choose and record one per purpose. The controller must prove consent (art. 8).
- **Sensitive data** (including health and biometrics): specific and highlighted consent, or one of the few other bases. Health data cannot be shared for economic advantage, with limited exceptions.
- **Children under 12**: specific consent from a parent. **Estatuto Digital da Criança e do Adolescente (ECA Digital)** (Lei 15.211/2025, in force from 17 March 2026): extra duties for digital products likely to be used by people under 18 (age verification, default privacy, parental tools). Fines can be much higher than LGPD fines (verify).
- **Encarregado (DPO)**: required. Small businesses and startups can be exempt but must offer a communication channel (ANPD Resolution 2/2022).
- **Breach**: notify the ANPD and data subjects within **3 business days** when the incident can cause relevant risk or damage (ANPD Resolution 15/2024).
- International transfers (art. 33): ANPD Resolution 19/2024 (adequacy, standard contractual clauses, specific clauses). The adaptation period ended 23 August 2025, so contracts with foreign providers (for example US cloud hosting) must already include the ANPD standard clauses.
- **Cookies**: the ANPD cookie guide (2022) says non-necessary cookies generally need consent. The banner must allow rejection, with no pre-selected options.
- Fines: up to 2% of revenue in Brazil, limited to BRL 50 million per violation.

**Consumer law**

- Código de Defesa do Consumidor (CDC), Lei 8.078/1990. Authorities: Procon (state level) and Senacon. Platform: consumidor.gov.br.
- **Direito de arrependimento** (art. 49): **7 calendar days** from the contract or from receipt of the product, for purchases outside the store (including online). Full refund, including shipping, with monetary correction.
- **Decreto 7.962/2013** (e-commerce): show the seller's legal name, CNPJ, physical and electronic address on the site; summary of the contract before purchase; confirm receipt of the order; clear means for withdrawal; answer requests within 5 days.
- All consumer information must be in Portuguese.

## 5. Argentina

**Data protection**

- Law: Ley 25.326 (2000) and Decreto 1558/2001. Regulator: Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP), https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip. A new data protection bill has been discussed for years (verify status).
- Consent: free, express, and informed, in writing or an equivalent form.
- **Database registration** in the Registro Nacional de Bases de Datos at the AAIP (verify current procedure).
- Privacy notices must include the AAIP legend about the right to complain (Disposición 10/2008) (verify text).
- Argentina has EU adequacy status. International transfers need adequate protection, contracts, or consent.
- Breach notification: no general legal duty in the current law, but recommended. The reform bill proposes 72 hours.
- Fines in Ley 25.326 (art. 31) are outdated amounts, but the AAIP can also suspend or close databases.

**Consumer law**

- Ley 24.240 de Defensa del Consumidor and the Código Civil y Comercial (arts. 1110 and following).
- **Revocación**: **10 calendar days** from delivery or the contract, whichever is later. Cannot be waived. The seller pays return costs.
- **Botón de arrepentimiento**: a prominent link on first access (home page or first app screen) to revoke the purchase, with no prior registration or extra steps. Give a request code within 24 hours. Current rule: **Disposición 954/2025** (Subsecretaría de Defensa del Consumidor, in force 4 September 2025), which replaced Resolución 424/2020.
- **Botón de baja de servicio** (Disposición 954/2025): a link with that exact label, same placement, to cancel services. Give a request code within 24 hours and complete the cancellation through the same channel.

## 6. Chile

**Data protection**

- Old law: Ley 19.628 (1999). New law: **Ley 21.719** (published 13 December 2024), in force from **1 December 2026**. A bill (boletín 18.623-07) proposes to postpone it to 1 December 2027 (verify status). New regulator: Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales.
- Design for Ley 21.719 now. The launch date of most startups falls after the effective date.
- The new law is close to GDPR: legal bases (consent, contract, legal duty, legitimate interest), information duties, rights (access, rectification, deletion, opposition, portability, blocking), breach notification to the Agency and to data subjects in some cases, and a compliance program ("modelo de prevención de infracciones") that can reduce fines.
- Children under 14: consent from parents. Adolescents 14 to 16 can consent for non-sensitive data (verify).
- Breach: notify the Agency without undue delay ("sin dilaciones indebidas"), and data subjects in some cases.
- Fines: tiers of 5,000, 10,000, and 20,000 UTM, and up to 4% of annual revenue for large companies on repeat violations (verify).

**Consumer law**

- Ley 19.496 de Protección de los Derechos de los Consumidores, as amended by Ley 21.398 (2021, "Ley Pro Consumidor"). Authority: SERNAC, https://www.sernac.cl.
- **Derecho de retracto** for remote purchases (art. 3 bis): **10 days** from receipt of the product or the contract. Cannot be waived for goods since Ley 21.398 (verify rules for services and digital content). Refund deadline: sources conflict (verify).
- The seller must show its identity, total price, and a contract confirmation by email.

## 7. Peru

**Data protection**

- Law: Ley 29733 (2011). New regulation: **Decreto Supremo 016-2024-JUS**, in force from 30 March 2025. Regulator: Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales (ANPD, Ministerio de Justicia).
- The new regulation also applies to foreign businesses that offer goods or services to people in Peru.
- Fines: new calculation method in RM 0476-2025-JUS. Very serious infractions up to 100 UIT (verify).
- Consent: free, prior, express, informed, and unequivocal. Written for sensitive data.
- **Database registration** in the Registro Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales (verify changes in the new regulation).
- International transfers: inform the ANPD (verify procedure).
- Breach: notify the ANPD within **48 hours** (new regulation) and data subjects when relevant (verify).
- DPO ("Oficial de Datos Personales"): required for some controllers under the new regulation (verify criteria).
- Minors: 14 and older can consent, except where the law requires parents.

**Consumer law**

- Código de Protección y Defensa del Consumidor, Ley 29571. Authority: INDECOPI.
- **Libro de Reclamaciones virtual**: mandatory for businesses that sell online. A visible link on the site.
- Right of withdrawal for remote sales: no clear general right found. INDECOPI has proposed a 15-day right for e-commerce (verify status). Use the user's default or the longest period of the other target countries.

## 8. Other countries

Ecuador (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales, 2021, in force 2023), Uruguay (Ley 18.331, EU adequacy), Costa Rica (Ley 8968), Panama (Ley 81 de 2019), Paraguay (Ley 7593/2025, verify), Dominican Republic, Guatemala, and others. Use the method in `other.md`.

## 9. Checklist

- [ ] Privacy Policy / Política de Tratamiento / Aviso de Privacidad in the local language with the content each law requires.
- [ ] Short privacy notice at each point of collection (forms).
- [ ] Separate, unchecked consent checkboxes. Express (written) consent for sensitive data.
- [ ] Consent evidence stored (see `../consent-records.md`).
- [ ] Database registration where required (Colombia RNBD if over the threshold, Argentina, Peru).
- [ ] DPO or data protection channel (Brazil, Peru).
- [ ] International transfers listed and covered.
- [ ] Cookie consent banner for non-necessary cookies (opt-in is the safe default).
- [ ] Right of withdrawal: use the longest period of the target countries. Easy withdrawal channel. Argentina "botón de arrepentimiento" and "botón de baja de servicio".
- [ ] Subscriptions: renewal notice and immediate online cancel (Mexico art. 76 Bis, Argentina botón de baja).
- [ ] Seller identity and contact visible on the site (legal name, tax ID, address).
- [ ] Link to the consumer authority where required (SIC, PROFECO, Procon, SERNAC, INDECOPI). Peru "Libro de Reclamaciones".
- [ ] Marketing contact rules (Colombia Ley 2300, Mexico REPEP).
- [ ] Breach response plan with each country's deadline (Brazil 3 business days, Peru 48 hours, Colombia 15 business days).
