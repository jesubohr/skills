# Intake Questionnaire

Ask only the questions that the project inspection did not answer. Ask in groups, in this order. Offer default answers where you can, so the user can confirm fast.

Explain why a question matters when it is not obvious. Users answer better when they know the reason.

## Group 1: Legal identity (required for all documents)

1. Legal name of the business or person that owns the product. Is it a registered company or a person?
2. Tax or registration ID (for example NIT, RFC, CNPJ, CUIT, RUT, RUC, EIN).
3. Country, city, and physical address of the business.
4. Contact email for legal and privacy requests. Is there a phone number?
5. Is there a person responsible for data protection (Data Protection Officer or "Oficial de Protección de Datos")?
6. Brand or product name, and the domain(s) and app store names.

## Group 2: Product and market

1. What does the product do, in one or two sentences?
2. Who are the users: consumers (B2C), businesses (B2B), or both?
3. In which countries will users live? Will you actively target them (local language, local currency, local ads, local shipping)?
4. In the United States, which states, or all?
5. Can minors use the product? What is the minimum age?
6. Languages of the website or app.
7. Is the sector regulated? (health, finance, insurance, education, legal, gambling, crypto, alcohol, transport, children)

## Group 3: Money

1. Do users pay? For what: physical goods, digital goods, services, subscriptions, or in-app purchases?
2. Payment provider (for example Stripe, Mercado Pago, PayU, Wompi, PayPal, Apple, Google).
3. For subscriptions: free trial? auto-renewal? How does a user cancel?
4. Refund rule you want. Default is 5 days after purchase. Confirm or change. Explain that the law can require more.
5. Are there exceptions you want (for example digital content already downloaded, custom products, services already delivered)?
6. Who pays return shipping for physical goods?

## Group 4: Personal data

1. Which data do you collect, and why do you need each field? (Use the list from the project inspection and ask about the purpose of each field.)
2. Do you collect sensitive data: health, biometrics, exact location, race or ethnicity, religion, sexual life, political opinions, union membership, government IDs, financial account data, data of minors?
3. Do you use data for marketing (emails, SMS, push, ads, retargeting)?
4. Do you share or sell data to other companies? Which ones and why?
5. Third-party services that receive personal data (hosting, database, email, analytics, CRM, support chat, AI providers). Where are their servers?
6. How long do you keep data after a user deletes their account?
7. Do you use AI or automated decisions that affect users (for example scoring, approvals, pricing)?
8. Do users upload content (photos, posts, reviews, files)?

## Group 5: Content and media

1. Where did the images, videos, icons, fonts, and audio come from? (own work, stock sites, AI-generated, designers, the internet)
2. Do you have licenses or receipts for stock media?
3. For work from designers or freelancers: is there a contract that transfers the rights to you?
4. Do photos show identifiable people? Do you have their written permission (model release)?
5. Do you use third-party trademarks or logos (for example "as seen in", partner logos)?

## Group 6: Preferences

1. Where should the draft go? Default: `docs/legal-compliance/`.
2. Do you already have a lawyer who will review the documents?
3. Effective date for the documents. Default: the launch date, or today.
